- **3.5 Background paper — "Waves of Conservation in PNG"** — commissioned to accompany the symposium.
  Architecture approved, research complete, **drafting not yet commenced.** Target 4,000–6,000 words,
  scientific but practitioner-accessible. **Not yet a file — write from scratch.** Four-wave structure:
  1. Pre-contact & customary — conservation-adjacent behaviour was *contextually functional, not
     intentionally conservationist* (Sekhran 1996 / Bulmer 1982). Do **not** romanticise or map
     pre-contact practice onto contemporary conservation as directly applicable.
  2. Colonial & post-independence protected areas (1970s–1990s) — first national parks 1970s excluded
     communities → resentment/degradation; WMAs as community-controlled alternative; 1975 Constitution
     embedded conservation + customary land ownership (97% customary); by mid-1990s ~56 PAs, ~2.8% of land.
  3. ICDP / ICAD era (1986–2010s) — introduced by WWF, spread via UNDP/GEF. PNG projects: Crater Mountain,
     Lak (failed), Bismarck-Ramu (failed → BRG breakaway), Oro Butterfly, Kikori Basin, Kamiali, Hunstein.
     Common failures: cargo expectations, could not out-compete logging royalties, superficial cooperation,
     dependency/passivity, youth alienation. **MCA is NOT an ICAD.**
  4. Current — community conservation, 30x30 — YUS CA (TKCP, 2009, first gazetted CA), MCA (2017), WCS
     conservation deeds, MENCERTC. PNG Protected Areas Act 2024 targets 30x30; PNG ~4% coverage vs global 17.6%.
     Open questions: Are current approaches genuinely different from the ICDP wave? What would 30x30 require
     in a customary-land context? Who decides?
  - **Reference list & accessibility:** see `docs/MOPP_ClaudeCode_Handover_v1.docx` §3.5a (Sekhran 1996,
    Anderson 2005, Fisher et al. 2008, PNG PA Act 2024, IUCN 30x30 Guide 2023 are OPEN ACCESS; several
    grey-lit / paywalled sources listed there).

## 6. Operating principles and working style

- **No fabrication, ever.** Will consistently catches invented claims, unsupported citations, and unverified
  figures. Flag all uncertainty. Do not assert data from memory without re-reading the source file.
- **No edits without explicit go-ahead.** Will batches changes and gives one clear instruction ("Let's Go" or
  equivalent) before implementation. Do not implement piecemeal.
- **End-to-end verification.** Catch overflowing tables, section-numbering drift, embedded instruction text,
  and cross-reference errors proactively.
- **Source discipline.** Primary and peer-reviewed sources only. Untraceable historical PNG figures
  (1970s–80s) cannot be cited unless traceable to primary sources.
- **Present outputs as actionable** — no conditional gates, phasing language, or sequencing that makes the
  programme appear staged or dependent.
- **Standardisation is document-wide,** not just the edited section.
- **Prefer direct prose** over bullet-heavy formatting; no donor jargon or citation-dropping.
- **Version-numbered filenames are mandatory** on every output.
